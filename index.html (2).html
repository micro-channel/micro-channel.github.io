How these figures are built
  • Digital GWP = AISP receipts from the Micro Channel GWP report (by effective date, credited to the policy's advisor in the policy master) plus FDIP premium for OP sales codes from the GWP FD Policy-wise report (by commencement date, credited by sales code). UK-prefixed policies are excluded from Digital and counted in Postal.
  • MSME channel = all Business Class GLMB premium and Dialog = Business Class Dialog in the GLMB report, by invoice Gen Date. Advisor rows: Asela = OP901 and OP132; Jude = OP907 and OP908.
  • Postal GWP = UK policies from the Micro Channel GWP report plus US postal receipts from the Postal (Shakthi) workbook. US receipts are booked in their postal cycle month; receipts dated before the cycle month start on its 1st. Postal Fund Balance (Average) = the average of the daily total fund balance (each agent's UK and US codes) over the days in the period that have balances.
  • Digital Wealth figures come from the manually prepared Digital Wealth performance sheet (monthly, booked on the month's last day). M1 = June 2026. In the channel summary, Digital Wealth achievement counts in the Total but its target does not. Channel ANBP target = the Manager ANBP target; channel savings target = the AISP channel target; advisor ANBP targets follow each advisor's role.
  • Targets: a month's full target counts for every month the range touches, as in the Performance Monitoring sheet. Tick "Pro-rata targets" to scale part months by days.
  • Avg FB increase = average daily fund balance for the end month of the range up to the To date, minus the average of the month before the range starts. Its target is the monthly target times the months covered.
  • Monthly Performance shows the month of the To date, from the 1st of that month to the To date.
